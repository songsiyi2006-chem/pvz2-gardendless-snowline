**Choosing a version.** Use 0.2 or Revision 2 to carry previous progress forward. The first public beta used a fresh profile and a 17-second expedition opening. Each old release still has its own guide and video. The 0.2 guide describes current play.

## Local revisions between R2 and 0.2

- **R3** added a reversible Compatibility mode for scripted objectives. Saved progress carries through switching and restarting the stage.
- **R4** added at least six siege titans to each expedition, four interceptable rocket types, new plant ultimates and expanded aircraft payloads. Standard expedition setup became 13 game seconds.
- **R5** introduced the original Tyrant and Nightmare rules, darker selector and battle indicators, enemy and reward multipliers, and five distinct Mega Gatling projectile sprites.
- **0.2** is the first public release to include these local revisions. It adds the third super-plant family, favorites, entry fees, planting boosts, full Nightmare mastery, solar mastery and another air and armor balance pass. [Detailed 0.2 changelog](CHANGELOG-0.2.en.md)

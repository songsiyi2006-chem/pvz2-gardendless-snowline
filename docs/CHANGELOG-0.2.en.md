# Changes in 0.2

[简体中文](CHANGELOG-0.2.md) · [Home](https://github.com/songsiyi2006-chem/pvz2-gardendless-snowline/blob/v0.2/README.en.md) · [Earlier releases and downloads](https://github.com/songsiyi2006-chem/pvz2-gardendless-snowline/blob/v0.2/docs/HISTORY.en.md)

This record starts from the previous public release, **v0.1-r2**. R3, R4 and R5 were local revisions. Version 0.2 brings them to a public release for the first time.

## 0.2 · Seed selection, entry and mastery

1. **Citron joins the super plants.** Citron, Mega Gatling Pea and Tile Turnip are three distinct families. Bring one in the extra slots for Tyrant, or any two different families for Nightmare. The seven supplied cards do not raise the tier. Tile colors form one family. Imitater counts as the plant to its left.
2. **Owned reinforcements can be favorited.** Save frequently used plants and pick them quickly in seed selection. Super plants stay at the top. The briefing previews the resulting challenge tier.
3. **Each formal Tyrant or Nightmare attempt costs 30 diamonds.** Inspecting, changing or canceling a configuration is free. If the balance is below 30, the seed chooser stays open. Starting a retry incurs a fresh fee. Repeated start clicks charge once per battle. Practice runs do not debit the campaign wallet.
4. **Every card has a planting boost in Tyrant and Nightmare.** Newly planted plants with a native boost can activate it without consuming previously purchased boosts. Mega Gatling Pea still pays 100 sun for a successful activation.
5. **Nightmare gives the entire team mastery IV.** Every plant with a dedicated design plays at temporary rank six with mastery IV in its selected skill. This includes reinforcements and copied plants. Permanent ranks and mastery purchases remain on the account. In Iron Tyrant, the seven supplied plants share four I, two III and one IV, and owned reinforcements retain their own progression. Extra compatible plants use their rank-six numerical bonuses in Nightmare.
6. **Citron can trigger its ultimate naturally.** Each actual regular shot has an independent 1% chance. The normal projectile still fires. The ultimate cannot trigger another ultimate. Citron costs 600 sun before simulation.

## 0.2 · Garden training and sun production

- Each permanent rank-up adds **0.3% of base action speed**, **10% of base health** and **0.01 to the critical damage multiplier** across the garden. Accumulated training also benefits borrowed expedition plants. Borrowing a rank or copying a plant adds no permanent steps.
- Dedicated producers gain a separate sun-yield multiplier from their selected mastery. Levels I–IV give **1.25×, 1.45×, 1.70× and 3.00×**. At equal garden training with simulation off, a rank-six mastery-IV Sunflower drop is **3.12×** its rank-five value. Sky sun keeps its native value.
- Solar Harvest, Light-Screen Rescue and Corona Interception specialize in extra sun, shielding nearby plants and local interception. Select the protection your economy needs as the waves build.

## 0.2 · Nightmare armor and aircraft

- Nightmare helmets gain **1.75× durability**, and vests gain **2×**. Compared with the previous Nightmare armor, direct damage that passes a piece is multiplied by 0.70 and blast damage that passes it by 0.60. These pieces also resist direct armor stripping. Against an uncamouflaged light zombie with an intact helmet and vest, about 6.6 of 100 ordinary direct damage reaches its body.
- Each powered F22 adds 2% plant damage, 0.05 to the critical multiplier and 0.5 percentage points of armor shred for all plants. Shred normally caps at 5%. **Nightmare raises the cap to 12%.** Powered aircraft share one reconnaissance bonus of 12 percentage points of critical chance and 15% action speed. A fighter that lacks upkeep stops firing and withdraws its own bonuses.
- F35 guided hits gain base damage and armor wear. Clusters cover groups of zombies and incendiary tiles continue to deal damage each second. In Nightmare, a guided attack can reach a second lane. The level-one main hit has 5,100 damage and 1,050 direct armor wear. The secondary hit has 3,100 damage and 750 wear.
- The Air Fortress covers the lawn and cycles guided, bunker-buster and airburst attacks. A bunker-buster direct hit inflicts 6,500 base armor wear. Airbursts bypass the screening that shield guards and titans extend to other zombies. In Nightmare, an airburst can reach a second group outside its first blast. The F35 and Air Fortress use 80% of their normal salvo interval in Nightmare. The two Fortress calls still cost 20,000 and 40,000 sun.
- The 35-page Chinese-language illustrated guide and a two-minute 1080p silent gameplay trailer accompany this release. The guide explains shields, camouflage, mortars, ten high-threat plant zombies and titan rockets through their positions, tells and impact patterns. ## Local revisions included since 0.1 R2

### R3 · Compatibility mode for scripted stages

- Open **Mod & Compatibility** from the main menu or a battle's pause menu. Confirming ends the current attempt and returns to the main menu. Retry the objective under compatibility rules and switch the mod back on afterward.
- Both rulesets share saved stage progress, ranks and equipment. The separate holographic effects switch works during an active battle.

### R4 · Siege titans and aircraft rework

- Every expedition schedules at least six siege titans. Each carries a rocket rack in place of its imp, plus a heavy shield, reactive and liquid armor, an AI helmet and active protection. Intact armor absorbs around 99% of explosive and piercing damage and prevents Citron's original instant removal. Remaining titans must be dealt with before victory.
- Four rocket types cause blast damage, freezing, toxin damage or persistent burns. Interceptors can stop them in flight. The titan model shows its visor, shield, armor and rocket rack. Warning zones distinguish the incoming types.
- Mega Gatling Pea costs an extra 550 sun to plant and has a 5% automatic Plant Food chance. A successful activation costs 100 sun. Automatic activation checks funds, while a manually fed leaf may put the balance below zero. Its ultimate draws from cherry, electric, violet flame, plasma and piercing rounds.
- Citron's ultimate becomes a nuclear electric orb with 30% explosive-resistance bypass, armor fracture and six pulses of corrosive radiation true damage. The tile remains plantable afterward.
- Standard expedition setup fell from 17 seconds to 13. Extra starting sun rose from 300 to 1,000. Sun-producer simulation now reaches 12×. Ordinary plants still cap at 5×.
- F22 fighters fire both piercing and explosive fragment streams. Piercing briefly weakens zombie defenses, while fragments splash and chain. The F35 has distinct guided, cluster and persistent incendiary attacks. The Fortress gains guided bombs, bunker-busters and airbursts that can reach the zombies behind allied shields.

### R5 · Original Iron Tyrant and Nightmare

- Mega Gatling Pea and Tile Turnip were the original super families. Each family brought in an extra slot multiplies reinforced enemy damage and body health by 2 and speed by 1.3, and subtracts three seconds from setup. Resistance to blasts and armor stripping also increases. Version 0.2 adds Citron to the same rule.
- The ten high-threat plant zombies use half their usual windup and attack interval in Tyrant and one quarter in Nightmare. The briefing and combat overlays gained darker visual indicators for these tiers.
- Tyrant uses 1.5× the basic expedition reward weight and Nightmare uses 2×. Fixed payouts are respectively 15,000 / 20,000 coins, 30 / 40 diamonds and 90 / 120 rank points. Tyrant awards one random plant roll plus a 50% chance at a second. Nightmare guarantees two.
- Every Tile Turnip color is locked to 1× simulation. Mega Gatling's ultimate replaces the ordinary projectile sprite with one of five distinct in-flight illustrations.
